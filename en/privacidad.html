Privacy

What the app stores, which permissions it asks for, what this site does and what its host logs.

The AM app

  • Without an account, everything stays on your phone: your reminders and settings don’t leave it.
  • The account is optional. You can sign in with email and password or with Google. With an account, your reminders sync with Firebase (Google) and each account can only access its own data. You sign out or delete the account from the app; if you no longer have it, see how to ask us to delete it.
  • The language and the way of reminding you that you choose stay on your phone: they don’t go to the cloud or into backups.
  • No ads, no analytics and no location. AM doesn’t send your reminders to artificial intelligence services.
  • If you save a link, a phone number or an address as a quick action, AM only opens it when you tap it.

Permissions

Each permission is requested only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Notifications, for the alerts. AM also uses exact alarms so they arrive on time (Android doesn’t ask for confirmation for that) and schedules them again when you restart your phone.
  • The alarm rings with your phone’s alarm tone and volume. AM doesn’t change your volume or “Do not disturb”.
  • Camera, to take photos of documents and receipts.
  • Microphone, for dictation.
  • Contacts, only to read birthdays. AM can’t write to your contacts.
  • Calendar, only to read your events. The write permission is blocked: AM never changes your calendar.
  • Fingerprint or PIN, if you turn on the lock to open AM.

Photos and dictation

  • Photos are stored only on your phone. They don’t go to the cloud or into backups.
  • The text in a photo is read on the phone, with ML Kit from Google Play Services. The photo isn’t sent anywhere.
  • Dictation uses Android’s speech recognition, the same one as the keyboard’s microphone. Depending on the phone, it may process your voice on Google’s servers.

Backups

  • They’re files you create from Settings, or the weekly automatic backup to the folder you choose (Drive, Downloads…). You decide where to keep them and who to share them with.

This site

  • It’s a static page. It doesn’t use cookies, analytics, ad pixels or forms that send data.
  • If you choose a language or light or dark mode, that preference is saved only in your browser.
  • The demo runs inside your browser: what you type isn’t sent anywhere, isn’t saved and is cleared when you reload the page. It doesn’t schedule alerts. If you tap the microphone to dictate, your browser asks for permission and turns your voice into text with its own service, which may run on Google, Microsoft or Apple servers depending on the browser. AM doesn’t receive or store the audio.
  • The links to Instagram take you to its site, which has its own privacy rules. If you send us a direct message, for example to join the test, we only use that conversation to reply to you.

Contact

If you have questions about your data or want us to delete your account, email us at amapp.contacto@gmail.com.

The service that hosts the site

The site is published on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub logs technical data about visits, such as the IP address, to run and protect the service. GitHub handles that under its privacy statement; AM doesn’t receive or look at those logs.
