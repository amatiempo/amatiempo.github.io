What’s new

What’s new in
version 1.4.

Real alarms, three ways to get reminded, three languages and a welcome that gets everything ready. It comes with the ten improvements from 1.3: fifteen changes in all.

  • Featured

    A real alarm

    It rings with your phone’s alarm tone until you tap “Stop”, even with AM closed and the screen off. If you don’t answer it, it goes quiet after a minute and stays pending. “Snooze” rings it again in 10 minutes, without opening the app.

    Example: Pay the credit card Ringing · Stop · Snooze

  • Featured

    You choose how it reminds you

    Classic: each reminder uses what you chose for it. Notifications only. Or alarms only, so nothing slips by: before you turn it on, AM tells you how many alarms would ring in the next 7 days.

    ClassicNotificationsAlarms Example: With “Alarms only”, 4 alarms would ring in the next 7 days.
  • Featured

    In your language

    Spanish, English or Brazilian Portuguese, or your phone’s language. And it understands what you type or dictate in all three.

    For example, it understands the same thing in these three phrases:Mañana a las 9Tomorrow at 9Amanhã às 9

  • Featured

    A welcome that gets everything ready

    Seven short steps: your name, how you want to be reminded, the permissions it needs (with “Set it up with me”), your contacts and calendar if you want, and the look. If you close it, it picks up where you left off. And a six-step tour shows you how to use it.

    Step 3 of 7 · Set up alerts
  • Featured

    If something’s wrong, it tells you

    If something blocks your alerts, the home screen shows it on a red card and helps you fix it. “Test alarm” really rings and asks whether you heard it: AM doesn’t take for granted what it can’t check.

    Example: Alarms can’t ring Set up now

Plus everything from 1.3

  • Spaces

    A new tab brings together People, Payments, Documents and Places, each with its status: “3 birthdays · next one Sat, Oct 10”.

  • 29 categories, with search

    Trip, Exam, Pets, Vehicle, Medication and more. Search “bill” and Payment shows up; “flight”, Trip.

  • Ideas to show up prepared

    Depending on the occasion, AM suggests up to three steps, like buying the gift or checking the bookings. They’re only added if you tap them.

  • Saved preparations

    Save the steps for an occasion and reuse them with one tap, without repeating the ones already there.

  • The exact time

    Type 07:17 and it stays 07:17. No clock dials, no rounding.

  • Before you change a date

    You see how each preparation step moves before saving, and you can undo it afterwards.

  • Drafts that don’t get lost

    If you close the app halfway through, what you were writing will be waiting for you.

  • Sounds and vibration

    Each type of alert with its status in Android and a button to test it.

  • Clearer settings

    Grouped, with the status of each thing in plain sight.

  • Contacts and calendar, fixed

    Your contacts’ birthdays and your calendar events are now read correctly, with read-only permission.

Everything it does

The usual,
still here.

What’s new adds to all of this, which works just as before.

  • Photo → reminder

    Take a photo of a bill, a printed appointment or a document, and AM reads the title, date, time and amount. The photo is read on your phone: you review and save.

    Example: Pay Edesur S.A. Due 10/15 · $ 25,300.50 · with the photo as a receipt

  • Alert status

    It checks everything on your phone that could block an alert (permission, muted categories, battery saver, pause) and walks you through fixing it. It runs an end-to-end test and tells you when the last alert arrived.

    Example: All set to remind you Permission · Categories · Battery · Not paused

  • Payments, for real

    What you actually paid each time, even when the amount changes. How each bill is trending, the next 3 months, “Copy alias / CBU” and the receipt photo.

    Example: Electricity is up 18% since August

  • People and gifts

    For each birthday or anniversary, jot down gift ideas during the year and what you gave. You can import birthdays from your contacts.

    Example alert: Ana’s birthday · Tomorrow Ideas: book · Last year: perfume ($ 18,000)

  • Documents and expiry dates

    ID, passport, driver’s license, vehicle inspection, insurance or warranties, with alerts 2 months, 1 month and 1 week before, the “To renew” list and a photo of the document at hand.

    Example: Driver’s license Expires in 21 days · Tue, Oct 20

  • Voice dictation

    Tap the microphone and say “tomorrow at 10 dentist appointment” (in Spanish).

  • Packing lists

    Check items off each time and save them to reuse: trip, doctor, gym. It also flags what was left unchecked last time.

  • “Leave now”

    Save how long it takes you to get to each place and AM tells you when to leave, with “Directions”. It doesn’t use your location.

  • Your week

    A weekly alert with what’s coming (things, payments, birthdays and what to prepare) and a glance at next month.

  • Events from your calendar

    Brings in appointments and meetings from your calendar, Google Calendar included, and suggests what to prepare. Read-only: it never changes your calendar.

  • “I need to sort this out”

    Nudges you every 15 minutes, 1 hour or 3 hours, respects your quiet hours and alerts at most 3 times a day.

  • Several early alerts

    10 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month or 2 months before. And snoozing never moves the due date.

  • Buttons in the alert

    “Done”, “10 min” and “1 hour” work without opening the app.

  • Pause alerts

    For vacations: when you’re back you get a summary. There’s also a daily summary and a different sound per category.

  • Repeats like real life

    Every 2 weeks; Monday, Wednesday and Friday; the second Tuesday; the last Friday; the last business day; or “repeat 3 months after completing”.

  • Jot it down instantly

    A pinned button in the notification bar, icon shortcuts (New · Later · From a photo), sharing from another app and the “Today” widget.

  • Quick actions

    Call, open the payment, see the address, WhatsApp and share a reminder.

  • Your way

    It greets you by name. 6 colors, warm or neutral background, light, dark or automatic mode, and weeks starting on Monday or Sunday.

  • Your data, safe

    Optional account with sync, backups with preview and “Undo”, weekly automatic backup, fingerprint or PIN to open AM and “Undo” everywhere.

    FAQ

    What people usually ask.

    What can I remember with AM?

    Birthdays, payments, appointments, documents that expire, trips, exams, medication and anything with a date: there are 29 categories. Whatever has no date goes to “For later”.

    How much does it cost?

    Nothing. AM is free: no subscriptions, no ads and no in-app purchases.

    Which devices is it available on?

    On Android. It isn’t in the stores yet: we’re testing it with a few people and, if you’d like to join, write to us. The plan is to reach Google Play and the App Store; it doesn’t work on iPhone yet.

    Can it ring like an alarm clock?

    Yes, since 1.4. It rings with your phone’s alarm tone until you tap “Stop”, even with AM closed, and goes quiet by itself after a minute. Use it for whatever you choose, or switch everything to “Alarms only”.

    Do I need to create an account?

    No. It’s optional: it gives you a cloud copy and lets you use AM on more than one phone.

    Where are my reminders stored?

    Without an account, on your phone. With an account, also in Firebase (Google), and each account only sees its own. Photos always stay on your phone.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes: jotting things down, seeing your list and getting alerts all work offline. Syncing your account needs internet, and dictation may too, depending on the phone.

    What if an alert doesn’t arrive?

    Some phone brands restrict apps to save battery. “Alert status” checks what could block an alert, walks you through it with “Set it up with me” and tests it end to end. If something blocks your alerts, the home screen shows it on a red card.

    What language is the app in?

    In Spanish, English and Brazilian Portuguese. You pick it during the welcome or in Settings, or let it follow your phone’s language. What you write is never translated.

    What does it do with my photos, contacts and calendar?

    It only uses them when you use that feature. Photos are read on the phone and the calendar is never changed.

    Can I recover my data if I change phones?

    Yes: sign in with your account, or import a backup (a manual one or the weekly automatic one). Photos don’t transfer.

    How do I delete my account?

    From the app, in Settings › Account › Delete account. If you no longer have the app, Delete your account explains how to ask for it.

    Will this page’s demo send me alerts?

    No. The demo doesn’t schedule alerts or save anything: what you type stays in your browser and is cleared when you reload the page. It only asks for the microphone if you want to dictate.
